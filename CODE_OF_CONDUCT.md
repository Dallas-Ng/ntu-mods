# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language in issues, PRs, or discussions.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer via the contact on the [ntumods.com](https://ntumods.com) site. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributors that violate this.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), v2.1.

# Complete NTU BDE List: Every BDE Category and Module (2026)

If you are looking for a list of NTU BDEs, this is it. Below is a breakdown by BDE category with real module codes and links to check each one on NTUMods. Availability changes each semester, so always open the module page to verify before you plan around it.

Not sure what a BDE is or how many you need? Start with [What Is a BDE at NTU](/articles/what-is-a-bde-at-ntu). If you want to know which BDEs are most popular and why, see [Most Popular BDEs at NTU](/articles/most-popular-bdes-at-ntu).

## How to use this BDE list

This list is organised by category. Each module link goes to NTUMods where you can check:

- Whether the module is available in your target semester
- Prerequisites and exclusions that apply to your programme
- Assessment structure (exam-heavy vs coursework-only)
- Index times that fit your timetable

Not every module on this list is open to every student. Programme restrictions, prerequisites, and AU limits all apply. Always confirm a module counts as a BDE for your specific degree before registering.

## Language BDEs

Language BDEs are among the most consistently popular at NTU. They come in progressions — Level 1 leads to Level 2, and so on. Most students start at Level 1 unless they have formal prior study in the language.

| Module | Language | Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LA5001](/mods/LA5001) | Arabic | Level 1 |
| [LF5001](/mods/LF5001) | French | Level 1 |
| [LG5001](/mods/LG5001) | German | Level 1 |
| [LJ5001](/mods/LJ5001) | Japanese | Level 1 |
| [LK5001](/mods/LK5001) | Korean | Level 1 |
| [LM5001](/mods/LM5001) | Malay | Level 1 |

Japanese and Korean fill fastest — often in Round 1 of STARS. Higher levels (Level 2, Level 3) exist for most languages and have smaller cohorts that may not run if registration is low.

For what language BDEs actually feel like week to week, including attendance requirements and which language to choose, see [Language BDEs at NTU](/articles/language-bde-at-ntu).

## Computing and data BDEs

Computing BDEs are designed for non-computing students who want data and technical literacy exposure. Engineering students often underestimate the workload — being technically-minded does not cut the hours required.

| Module | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [BC2406](/mods/BC2406) | Analytics I: Visual & Predictive Techniques |
| [BS1009](/mods/BS1009) | Introduction to Computational Thinking |
| [CS2024](/mods/CS2024) | Web Design & Technologies |
| [SC5002](/mods/SC5002) | Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals & Applications |

BC2406 is the most-taken computing BDE but also one of the more demanding. Seats go quickly and the content is not trivial even for students with some technical background.

Design BDEs are project-based and mostly exam-free. A genuine change of pace in a semester heavy with technical exams and problem sets.

| Module | Description |
| --- | --- |

Project deadlines tend to cluster near the end of semester. Check when the major submission is due before registering — if your engineering or core deadlines also cluster late, the timing may still create a crunch.

## What to do once you have your shortlist

Registration strategy matters for popular modules. Japanese, Korean, BC2406, and BU5501 can fill in Round 1 of STARS. Having a backup shortlisted before Round 1 opens is not optional — it is standard practice.

See [How to Bid for a BDE at NTU](/articles/how-to-bid-for-bde-at-ntu) for a complete walkthrough of how STARS bidding works and what to do if your first choice fills up.

## How to use this list

Each module links to its NTUMods page where you can check:

- Whether it is offered this semester
- Prerequisites and exclusions for your programme
- Assessment structure and contact hours
- Available indexes that fit your timetable

Not every module is open to every student. Programme restrictions, prerequisites, and AU limits apply. Always confirm a module counts as a BDE for your specific degree before registering.

For the complete live list, use the [BDE filter on NTUMods](/mods) — select "BDE" under module type.

## Finding the full BDE list

This page covers the major BDE categories and representative modules. NTU has over 800 BDE modules in the current semester — far more than can be listed here comprehensively.

To browse everything:

1. Go to [NTUMods](/mods)
2. Under "Module Type", select **BDE**
3. Filter by keyword, school, or AU to narrow down

This gives you the complete live list with current availability, prerequisites, and assessment details for your semester.

Once you have your shortlist, see [How to Bid for a BDE at NTU](/articles/how-to-bid-for-bde-at-ntu) for STARS registration strategy. For help deciding which BDE type fits your semester, see [Easy BDEs at NTU](/articles/easy-bde-at-ntu).

# NTU Academic Calendar 2027/28

NTU Singapore has published an [indicative semester calendar for AY2027/28](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/media/docs/default-source/office-of-academic-services/ntu-academic-calendar-ay2027-28-%28semester%29_indicative.pdf). **The dates below are provisional.** NTU says it will confirm the calendar in February 2027.

## AY2027/28 dates at a glance

| Event | Semester 1 | Semester 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recess week | 27 September to 1 October 2027 | 28 February to 3 March 2028 |
| Revision and examination period | 15 November to 3 December 2027 | 17 April to 5 May 2028 |

The indicative calendar also lists **orientation activities from 19 July to 6 August 2027** and **University Welcome on 3 and 4 August 2027**.

## Where to confirm dates and plan your semester

- Check the [official NTU academic calendars page](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/matriculation/academic-calendars) for the latest version of the semester calendar. Use the relevant calendar if your programme runs on trimesters.
- Check your personal course registration slot in [NTU STARS](/articles/ntu-stars-guide) when registration information is available.
- Use the [NTU exam timetable](/exams) to check dates by module code once schedules for your semester are available. The periods above include revision time.

If you are planning the current academic year, see the [NTU Academic Calendar 2026/27](/articles/ntu-academic-calendar-ay2026).

# NTU AY2026/2027 Semester 2 Modules: What Is New and What Changed

AY2026/2027 Semester 2 module and class index data is now live on NTUMods. Semester 2 teaching begins on Monday 11 January 2027, so this is the right time to shortlist modules, compare indexes and test timetables before registration opens. This page goes through what is in the data, which schools changed the most, the modules that appear for the first time, and how the weekly timetable differs from Semester 1.

> **Start planning for Semester 2**
>
> Browse 1,145 modules, compare class indexes and build a clash-free timetable. NTUMods now defaults to AY2026/2027 Semester 2.
>
> [Browse Semester 2 modules](/mods "cta")

> **Quick summary:** 1,145 modules and 4,456 class indexes are offered. That is about the same number of modules as Semester 1 (1,139 modules and 4,604 indexes). 99 modules are new to the data. Art, Design & Media grew the most, while Communication Studies and NIE shrank the most. The Semester 2 exam timetable is now out: 394 modules sit exams from 26 April to 6 May 2027.

## Semester 2 at a glance

|                                         | AY2026/27 Sem 2 | AY2026/27 Sem 1 | AY2025/26 Sem 2 |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| Modules offered                         | 1,145           | 1,139           | 1,085           |
| Class indexes                           | 4,456           | 4,604           | 4,230           |
| Class slots (lectures, tutorials, labs) | 7,533           | 7,840           | n/a             |

Compared with the same semester last year, Semester 2 has 60 more modules and 226 more indexes. Compared with Semester 1, it has 6 more modules and 148 fewer indexes. Class slots count each type, day and time once per index, so a lecture split across several venues or teaching weeks is one slot.

How much of the list carries over:

|                                                                    | Modules |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------- |
| Also offered in AY2026/27 Semester 1                               | 409     |
| Offered in Semester 2 only (not in Semester 1)                     | 736     |
| Also offered in AY2025/26 Semester 2                               | 893     |
| New to NTUMods (never offered in any earlier semester in the data) | 99      |

Most modules run in only one semester each year, which is why only about 410 modules appear in both. If a module you wanted is missing, open its module page and check which semesters it has been offered in. Modules that ran in Semester 2 last year are the best guide: 893 of this semester's modules did.

## How the weekly timetable looks

This is how class slots fall across the week compared with Semester 1, which matters when you compare indexes.

| Day       | Class slots, Sem 2 | Class slots, Sem 1 |
| --------- | ------------------ | ------------------ |
| Monday    | 1,519              | 1,689              |
| Tuesday   | 1,656              | 1,634              |
| Wednesday | 1,649              | 1,589              |
| Thursday  | 1,560              | 1,646              |
| Friday    | 1,109              | 1,230              |

Monday to Thursday carry a similar load in both semesters, and Friday is the lightest day in both. Slots that start at 6pm or later are about the same, at 351 in Semester 2 and 329 in Semester 1. Use the day and time filters in the [Module Browser](/mods) to narrow your search.

By type, the 7,533 slots are made up of 3,130 tutorials, 2,594 lectures, 891 seminars, 885 labs and a small number of project and design sessions. Eight slots have no fixed day or time, which usually means online or flexible delivery. The [timetable planner guide](/articles/ntu-timetable-planner-guide) shows how those appear in your plan.

## How to plan your Semester 2 timetable

1. **Check the rules first.** Work out which requirements you still need from your [degree audit](/articles/ntu-degree-audit-guide), then read the [module selection guide](/articles/ntu-module-selection-guide).
2. **Shortlist modules.** Use the [Module Browser](/mods) and filter by school, level, day and time. Each module page shows prerequisites, exclusions and class indexes.
3. **Compare indexes.** Add modules to a plan in the [timetable planner](/timetable) and check for clashes. You can add your own commitments with [custom blocks](/articles/custom-courses-timetable-ntu).
4. **Follow the pre-registration workflow.** [How to Plan NTU Modules Before STARS Opens](/articles/how-to-plan-for-modules) and the [STARS guide](/articles/ntu-stars-guide) cover what to do when your first choice fills up.
5. **Plan BDE bids.** See [how to bid for BDEs](/articles/how-to-bid-for-bde-at-ntu) and when [allocation results are released](/articles/ntu-bde-allocation-results-release-date).
6. **Export your plan.** Calendar export now uses the Semester 2 start date and recess week.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does NTU Semester 2 AY2026/2027 start?

Teaching Week 1 begins on Monday 11 January 2027. Recess Week is 1 to 5 March 2027.

### How many modules does NTU offer in Semester 2 AY2026/2027?

NTUMods lists 1,145 modules and 4,456 class indexes for AY2026/2027 Semester 2.

### Why is a Semester 1 module missing from Semester 2?

Many modules are offered in only one semester each year. Open the module page on NTUMods to see every semester it has run. Modules that ran in Semester 2 last year are likely to run again.

### What are the new modules in Semester 2?

99 modules appear for the first time, led by the EEE laboratory modules and the Art, Design & Media first-year studios. The list above has the highlights.

### Are the Semester 2 exam dates available?

Yes. Exams run from 26 April to 6 May 2027 and cover 394 modules. Find yours on the [exam schedule](/exams), and use the Semester filter to show Semester 2 only.

### Why do some modules show 0 AU?

The new EEE laboratory modules are listed with 0 AU in NTU's data. Check your programme's requirements or ask your school for how they count towards your degree.

### Does my Semester 1 timetable carry over?

No. Plans are tied to the semester's class indexes, so build a fresh plan for Semester 2 in the [timetable planner](/timetable).

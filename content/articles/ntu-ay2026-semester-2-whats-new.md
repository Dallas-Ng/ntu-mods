# NTU AY2026/2027 Semester 2 Modules: What Is New and What Changed

AY2026/2027 Semester 2 module and class index data is now live on NTUMods. Semester 2 teaching begins on Monday 11 January 2027, so this is the right time to shortlist modules, compare indexes and test timetables before registration opens. This page goes through what is in the data, which schools changed the most, the modules that appear for the first time, and how the weekly timetable differs from Semester 1.

> **Start planning for Semester 2**
>
> Browse 1,092 modules, compare class indexes and build a clash-free timetable. NTUMods now defaults to AY2026/2027 Semester 2.
>
> [Browse Semester 2 modules](/mods "cta")

> **Quick summary:** 1,092 modules and 4,306 class indexes are offered. That is slightly fewer than Semester 1 (1,138 modules and 4,591 indexes). 61 modules are new to the data. Art, Design & Media grew the most, while Communication Studies and Sport Science and Management shrank the most. The Semester 2 exam timetable is not released yet.

Compared with the same semester last year, Semester 2 has 7 more modules and 76 more indexes. Compared with Semester 1, it has 46 fewer modules.

How much of the list carries over:

|                                                                    | Modules |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------- |
| Also offered in AY2026/27 Semester 1                               | 392     |
| Offered in Semester 2 only (not in Semester 1)                     | 700     |

| New to NTUMods (never offered in any earlier semester in the data) | 61      |

Most modules run in only one semester each year, which is why only about 390 modules appear in both. If a module you wanted is missing, open its module page and check which semesters it has been offered in. Modules that ran in Semester 2 last year are the best guide: 878 of this semester's modules did.

## How the weekly timetable looks

The shape of the week is different from Semester 1, which matters when you compare indexes.

| Day       | Class slots, Sem 2 | Class slots, Sem 1 |
| --------- | ------------------ | ------------------ |
| Monday    | 1,967              | 1,738              |
| Tuesday   | 2,120              | 1,690              |
| Wednesday | 2,155              | 1,650              |
| Thursday  | 2,004              | 1,794              |
| Friday    | 1,410              | 2,028              |

Semester 2 slots are spread more evenly from Monday to Thursday, with a lighter Friday. Slots that start at 6pm or later also fall from 1,050 in Semester 1 to 392. If you want a free Friday, you will have an easier time this semester. Use the day and time filters in the [Module Browser](/mods) to narrow your search.

By type, the 9,687 slots are made up of 3,707 tutorials, 3,562 lectures, 1,512 seminars, 873 labs and a small number of project and design sessions. Seven indexes have no fixed day or time, which usually means online or flexible delivery. The [timetable planner guide](/articles/ntu-timetable-planner-guide) shows how those appear in your plan.

## How to plan your Semester 2 timetable

1. **Check the rules first.** Work out which requirements you still need from your [degree audit](/articles/ntu-degree-audit-guide), then read the [module selection guide](/articles/ntu-module-selection-guide).
2. **Shortlist modules.** Use the [Module Browser](/mods) and filter by school, level, day and time. Each module page shows prerequisites, exclusions and class indexes.
3. **Compare indexes.** Add modules to a plan in the [timetable planner](/timetable) and check for clashes. You can add your own commitments with [custom blocks](/articles/custom-courses-timetable-ntu).
4. **Follow the pre-registration workflow.** [How to Plan NTU Modules Before STARS Opens](/articles/how-to-plan-for-modules) and the [STARS guide](/articles/ntu-stars-guide) cover what to do when your first choice fills up.
5. **Plan BDE bids.** See [how to bid for BDEs](/articles/how-to-bid-for-bde-at-ntu) and when [allocation results are released](/articles/ntu-bde-allocation-results-release-date).
6. **Export your plan.** Calendar export now uses the Semester 2 start date and recess week.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does NTU Semester 2 AY2026/2027 start?

Teaching Week 1 begins on Monday 11 January 2027. Recess Week is 1 to 5 March 2027.

### How many modules does NTU offer in Semester 2 AY2026/2027?

NTUMods lists 1,092 modules and 4,306 class indexes for AY2026/2027 Semester 2.

### Why is a Semester 1 module missing from Semester 2?

Many modules are offered in only one semester each year. Open the module page on NTUMods to see every semester it has run. Modules that ran in Semester 2 last year are likely to run again.

### What are the new modules in Semester 2?

61 modules appear for the first time, led by the Art, Design & Media first-year studios and the EEE laboratory modules. The list above has the highlights.

### Are the Semester 2 exam dates available?

Not yet. NTU has not released the Semester 2 exam timetable. The [exam schedule](/exams) shows Semester 1 exams for now and will update when the Semester 2 timetable is published.

### Why do some modules show 0 AU?

The new EEE laboratory modules are listed with 0 AU in NTU's data. Check your programme's requirements or ask your school for how they count towards your degree.

### Does my Semester 1 timetable carry over?

No. Plans are tied to the semester's class indexes, so build a fresh plan for Semester 2 in the [timetable planner](/timetable).

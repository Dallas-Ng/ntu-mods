# NTU AY2026/2027 Semester 2 Modules: What's New and What Changed

AY2026/2027 Semester 2 module and class index data is now live on NTUMods. Semester 2 teaching begins on Monday 11 January 2027, so this is the right time to shortlist modules and test timetables before registration. This page summarises what is in the data and what is different from Semester 1.

> **Start planning:** Browse the [Module Browser](/mods) or open the [timetable planner](/timetable). NTUMods now defaults to AY2026/2027 Semester 2.

## Semester 2 at a glance

| | AY2026/27 Semester 2 |
|---|---|
| Modules offered | 1,092 |
| Class indexes | 4,306 |
| Modules new to NTUMods (never offered in the data before) | 61 |
| Also offered in Semester 1 | 392 |
| Also offered in AY2025/26 Semester 2 | 878 |

Semester 1 had 1,138 modules and 4,591 indexes, so Semester 2 is a little smaller. That is normal: many modules run in only one semester, and only about 390 of this semester's modules also ran in Semester 1. If a module you wanted is missing, open its module page and check which semesters it has been offered in.

## What is new this semester

Some highlights from the 61 modules that appear for the first time in the data:

- **Art, Design & Media first-year studios.** A new set of foundation modules including Drawing: Line & Form ([DA1100](/mods/DA1100)), Typographic Form ([DC1100](/mods/DC1100)), Photography for Designers ([DC1102](/mods/DC1102)), Sight & Sound ([DF1100](/mods/DF1100)), Dynamic Drawing & Motion ([DT1100](/mods/DT1100)) and Gameplay ([DT1102](/mods/DT1102)). ADM has 82 modules this semester, up from 63 in Semester 1.
- **EEE laboratory modules.** Fifteen new lab modules with an `L` suffix, such as Circuit Analysis ([E2101L](/mods/E2101L)) and Signals & Systems ([E2110L](/mods/E2110L)). They carry 0 AU in the data, so check your programme requirements for how they count.
- **Communication in the Age of Generative AI** ([CS2060](/mods/CS2060)), from the Wee Kim Wee School of Communication and Information.
- **Language modules.** Including Portuguese Language Level 1 ([LP5001](/mods/LP5001)), Bahasa Indonesia Level 2 ([LB5002](/mods/LB5002)), German Language Level 6 ([LG5006](/mods/LG5006)) and Korean Language Level 8 ([LK5008](/mods/LK5008)). See [Language BDEs at NTU](/articles/language-bde-at-ntu) for how language modules fit into your BDE options.
- **A computer science capstone.** [SC3099](/mods/SC3099) Capstone Project.
- **History and humanities.** For example Environmental History of Modern Japan ([HH2003](/mods/HH2003)) and Cultural History of Late Imperial China ([HC4045](/mods/HC4045)).

## What is not out yet

- **Exam timetable.** NTU has not released the Semester 2 exam timetable, so [the exam schedule](/exams) still shows Semester 1. NTU normally releases it in mid November. See [NTU Exam Timetable and Seating](/articles/ntu-exam-timetable-seating-past-papers).
- **School and BDE guides.** Some guides, such as the [Complete NTU BDE List](/articles/complete-ntu-bde-list), still list Semester 1 modules while we refresh them for Semester 2. Module pages always show the live data.

## How to plan your Semester 2 timetable

1. Shortlist modules in the [Module Browser](/mods). Filter by school, level, day and time.
2. Add them to a plan in the [timetable planner](/timetable) and compare indexes for clashes. The [timetable planner guide](/articles/ntu-timetable-planner-guide) covers the workflow.
3. Check dates with the [NTU Academic Calendar AY2026/2027](/articles/ntu-academic-calendar-ay2026). Add/drop runs in Teaching Weeks 1 and 2, see the [add/drop guide](/articles/ntu-add-drop-period-guide).
4. Export your plan to your calendar. Teaching Week 1 and the March recess week now line up with Semester 2 dates.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does NTU Semester 2 AY2026/2027 start?

Teaching Week 1 begins on Monday 11 January 2027. Recess Week is 1 to 5 March 2027.

### How many modules does NTU offer in Semester 2 AY2026/2027?

NTUMods lists 1,092 modules and 4,306 class indexes for AY2026/2027 Semester 2.

### Why is a Semester 1 module missing from Semester 2?

Many modules are offered in only one semester each year. Open the module page on NTUMods to see every semester it has run.

# NTU Business Modules: Courses Offered by Nanyang Business School (AY2026/2027 Semester 1)

Nanyang Business School (NBS) is NTU's business faculty. It offers modules across accountancy, business management, and banking and finance. This page lists the NBS modules available in AY2026/2027 Semester 1 and explains how to browse them on NTUMods.

> **Browse all business modules on NTUMods:** Use the [Module Browser](/mods) and search by code prefix (`AB` for accountancy and business, `BF` for banking and finance) or by module title.

NBS modules use two main code prefixes:

- **AB** — Accountancy & Business: foundational and cross-disciplinary modules in management, marketing, statistics, law, and organisational behaviour
- **BF** — Banking & Finance: specialised modules in investments, corporate finance, commodities, fintech, and capital markets

Not all NBS modules are open to non-business students. Some are programme-restricted. Check the module page on NTUMods for restrictions, prerequisites, and availability.

## How to browse NTU business modules on NTUMods

The [Module Browser](/mods) shows all NTU modules for the current semester. To find business modules:

1. Go to [ntumods.com/mods](/mods)
2. Type `AB` or `BF` in the search box to filter by code prefix
3. Or search by topic — "marketing", "investments", "management"

Each module page shows prerequisites, exclusions, available indexes with timeslots and venues, and the exam date for the current semester. You can add modules directly to your [timetable](/timetable) to check for index clashes before STARS registration opens.

## Frequently asked questions

### What modules does NTU's Nanyang Business School offer?

NBS offers modules across accountancy and business (AB prefix), and banking and finance (BF prefix). AB modules cover management, marketing, law, statistics, and communication. BF modules cover investments, corporate finance, commodities, fintech, and capital markets. The full list for AY2026/2027 Semester 1 is above.

### Can non-business students take NTU business modules?

Some AB modules are open to non-business students as BDEs. Most BF modules are restricted to Banking & Finance and Accountancy programme students. Check the module page on [NTUMods](/mods) for programme restrictions before planning to register.

### How many NTU business modules are available in AY2026/2027 Semester 1?

NTUMods shows approximately 59 NBS modules (AB + BF) available for AY2026/2027 Semester 1. Not all are open to all students.

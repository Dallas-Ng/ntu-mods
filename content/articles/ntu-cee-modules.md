## Frequently asked questions

### What module prefix does CEE use?

Most CEE modules use the `CV` prefix. Some cross-listed environmental and urban systems modules appear under `EN` and `SU`. All are grouped under the CEE faculty filter on NTUMods.

### How many CEE modules does NTU offer in AY2026/2027 Semester 1?

NTUMods shows approximately 18 CEE modules for AY2026/2027 Semester 1. CEE cohorts are smaller than some other engineering schools, so the module list is shorter — check the [Module Browser](/mods?moduleFaculty=CEE) for the current live list.

### Are CEE modules available as BDEs for non-CEE students?

Some CEE modules may be open as BDEs depending on programme restrictions and semester. Check each module page on [NTUMods](/mods) for eligibility — see [Complete NTU BDE List](/articles/complete-ntu-bde-list) for BDE options across all faculties.

# NTU Degree Audit: How to Check Your Graduation Requirements

The degree audit is NTU's official record of what you have completed and what you still need to graduate. It is not the same as STARS, and it is not the same as your CGPA. This guide explains what it shows, where to find it, and how to actually use it when planning modules.

> **Quick summary:** The degree audit is accessed through the [NTU student portal](/articles/ntu-student-portal-links), not through STARS. It breaks your requirements into categories (core, BDE, UE, PE, and more), shows what is fulfilled versus outstanding, and should be checked before every STARS registration window — not just before graduation.

## What the degree audit actually shows

The degree audit maps every module you have taken, or are currently registered for, against your programme's graduation requirements. It groups requirements into categories such as:

- **Core / Programme requirements** — modules specific to your degree, usually with little flexibility
- **BDE (Broadening and Deepening Electives)** — see [What Is a BDE at NTU](/articles/what-is-a-bde-at-ntu) for how these work
- **UE (Unrestricted Electives)** — see [NTU Unrestricted Electives](/articles/ntu-unrestricted-electives)
- **CC (Common Core)** — see [NTU Common Core Modules](/articles/ntu-cc-modules-what-they-actually-feel-like)
- **PE (Prescribed Electives)** — programme-specific electives, requirements vary by school
- **Total AU** — your cumulative Academic Units against the AU required for your programme

For each category, the audit shows what is satisfied, what is in progress (registered but not yet graded), and what is still outstanding.

## Where to find your degree audit

The degree audit is not inside STARS. It is a separate report accessed through NTU's student portal system. See [NTU Student Links](/articles/ntu-student-portal-links) for the full set of portal links students use regularly, including where the degree audit sits alongside STARS, NTULearn, and CareerAxis.

If you cannot locate it, check with your school's academic office — the exact access path has changed between cohorts as NTU has updated its portal systems.

## Why the degree audit matters more than STARS availability

STARS will let you register for a module if seats are available and prerequisites are met. It does **not** check whether that module satisfies a graduation requirement for your specific programme. This distinction causes real problems:

- A module might be open to you in STARS as a BDE, but your school's requirements changed and it no longer counts
- A module might be pre-approved for one cohort's curriculum but excluded for a later cohort
- Programme restrictions on BDE/UE eligibility can differ even within the same school

The degree audit is the only place that reflects your actual requirement status. STARS availability and degree audit fulfilment are two separate checks — always confirm both. See [NTU STARS: How the Module Registration System Works](/articles/ntu-stars-guide) for how STARS registration works alongside this.

## How to read your degree audit before STARS opens

1. **Check outstanding categories first.** Identify which requirement buckets (core, BDE, UE, PE) still need modules, and how many AUs remain in each.
2. **Cross-check against your shortlist.** Before you finalise your STARS shortlist, confirm each module actually maps to an outstanding category — not just that it is available to bid for.
3. **Watch for "in progress" modules.** Modules you are currently taking show as in-progress, not yet fulfilled. If you are relying on a current module to satisfy a requirement, it will not show as complete until grades are released.
4. **Recheck after every semester.** Requirements update after grades post. Re-run your audit at the start of each planning cycle rather than relying on a stale mental model from a previous semester.

For the full pre-STARS planning workflow, including AU limits and prerequisite checks, see [How to Plan NTU Modules Before STARS Opens](/articles/how-to-plan-for-modules).

## Degree audit vs CGPA vs transcript

These three records answer different questions:

| Record | Answers |
|---|---|
| Degree audit | Have I met the requirements to graduate? |
| Transcript | What grade did I get in each module I have taken? |
| CGPA | What is my cumulative weighted average across graded modules? |

A module can appear complete on your transcript with a passing grade and still not satisfy a degree audit requirement, if it was taken outside the category your programme needs. Always check the degree audit specifically for graduation-requirement questions — the transcript and CGPA will not tell you that.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the degree audit the same as STARS?

No. STARS is the registration system for bidding on and adding modules. The degree audit is a separate report showing whether your completed and in-progress modules satisfy your programme's graduation requirements. Check both — STARS availability does not confirm a module counts toward your degree.

### How often should I check my degree audit?

Before every STARS registration window, and again after grades are released each semester. Requirements and your fulfilment status can shift, especially around BDE and UE categories where module eligibility changes between cohorts.

### What if a module I took doesn't show as fulfilling a requirement?

This usually means the module was not approved for that category under your specific programme's curriculum, even if it appeared available in STARS at the time. Contact your school's academic office to clarify — this needs to be resolved through your school, not through STARS.

### Does the degree audit show my CGPA?

Typically no — the degree audit focuses on requirement fulfilment, not grade-weighted averages. Check your transcript or the relevant grades portal for CGPA.

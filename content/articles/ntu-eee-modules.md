# NTU EEE Modules: Courses Offered by the School of Electrical & Electronic Engineering (AY2026/2027 Semester 1)

The School of Electrical & Electronic Engineering (EEE) is one of NTU's largest engineering schools. It offers two main module tracks — the core **EE**-prefix electrical engineering curriculum, and the **IE**-prefix track covering data science, AI, and information engineering. This page lists EEE modules available in AY2026/2027 Semester 1 and how to browse them on NTUMods.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between EE and IE modules at EEE?

EE-prefix modules cover the core electrical engineering curriculum — circuits, power, semiconductors, and control systems. IE-prefix modules cover information engineering, data structures, and the AI/data science electives that make EEE's later-year offerings popular even outside the school. Both tracks sit under the same faculty.

### How many EEE modules does NTU offer in AY2026/2027 Semester 1?

NTUMods shows approximately 69 EEE modules (EE + IE) available for AY2026/2027 Semester 1. Check the [Module Browser](/mods?moduleFaculty=EEE) for the current live count and availability, since offerings change each semester.

### Can non-EEE students take EEE modules as BDEs?

Some IE-prefix electives, particularly the AI and data science modules, are commonly available as BDEs to non-EEE students. Availability and restrictions vary by module and by your own programme — check each module page on [NTUMods](/mods) for BDE eligibility before planning to register.

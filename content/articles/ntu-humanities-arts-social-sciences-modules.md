## How to find art history modules at NTU

NTU does not offer a standalone Art History degree programme, but art history content appears in several modules across faculties:

- [HD0103](/mods/HD0103) — Digital Humanities: Art, Art History & Cultural Heritage (3 AU)
- AAA-prefix modules from NIE include art history context (see list above)
- Some HL and HH modules touch on visual culture and cultural history

For the most current course offerings, search "art history" in the [Module Browser](/mods).

## Frequently asked questions

### What English modules does NTU offer?

NTU's School of Humanities (part of CoHASS) offers English literature modules under the HL prefix and linguistics modules under the HG prefix. HL modules cover literary periods, genres, and critical theory. HG modules cover language structure, sociolinguistics, and computational linguistics. The full list for AY2026/2027 Semester 1 is above.

### Does NTU have an art history programme?

NTU does not offer a standalone Art History degree. Art history content is available in individual modules such as [HD0103](/mods/HD0103) Digital Humanities: Art, Art History & Cultural Heritage, and through NIE's visual arts electives (AAA prefix). History students at NTU can also take modules in cultural and visual history through the HH programme.

### What history modules does NTU currently offer?

NTU's history programme (HH prefix) covers Asian history, global history, environmental history, and social history. Current offerings for AY2026/2027 Semester 1 include 20 modules from HH1001 (What Is History?) to HH4092 (Special Topics In History). The full list is above.

### Can engineering or computing students take humanities modules?

Yes. Many CoHASS modules are open to all NTU students as BDEs. This includes modules from HL (literature), HG (linguistics), HH (history), and HY (philosophy). Check the module page on [NTUMods](/mods) for programme-specific restrictions before planning to register.

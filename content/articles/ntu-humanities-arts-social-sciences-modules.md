# NTU Humanities, Arts & Social Sciences Modules: English, History, Philosophy, Linguistics, Art (AY2026/2027)

NTU's College of Humanities, Arts, and Social Sciences (CoHASS) offers modules in English literature, linguistics, history, philosophy, art and design, and other humanities disciplines. This page lists all CoHASS modules available in AY2026/2027 Semester 2 organised by subject area.

> **Browse all CoHASS modules on NTUMods:** Use the [Module Browser](/mods) and search by code prefix, `HL` for literature, `HG` for linguistics, `HH` for history, `HY` for philosophy, `AAA` for visual arts.

Many CoHASS modules are available as BDEs open to students from other faculties. Module availability varies by programme, check the module page for restrictions.

## How to find art history modules at NTU

NTU does not offer a standalone Art History degree programme, but art history content appears in several modules across faculties:

- [HR2002](/mods/HR2002): Issues in Art History & Theory (3 AU)
- AAA-prefix modules from NIE include art history context (see list above)
- Some HL and HH modules touch on visual culture and cultural history

For the most current course offerings, search "art history" in the [Module Browser](/mods).

## Frequently asked questions

### What English modules does NTU offer?

NTU's School of Humanities (part of CoHASS) offers English literature modules under the HL prefix and linguistics modules under the HG prefix. HL modules cover literary periods, genres, and critical theory. HG modules cover language structure, sociolinguistics, and computational linguistics. The full list for AY2026/2027 Semester 2 is above.

### Does NTU have an art history programme?

NTU does not offer a standalone Art History degree. Art history content is available in individual modules such as [HR2002](/mods/HR2002) Issues in Art History & Theory, and through NIE's visual arts electives (AAA prefix). History students at NTU can also take modules in cultural and visual history through the HH programme.

### What history modules does NTU currently offer?

NTU's history programme (HH prefix) covers Asian history, global history, environmental history, and social history. Current offerings for AY2026/2027 Semester 2 include 23 modules from HH1003 (Asia-Pacific in Global History: From 1800) to HH4091 (Special Topics in History- Interdisciplinary History). The full list is above.

### Can engineering or computing students take humanities modules?

Yes. Many CoHASS modules are open to all NTU students as BDEs. This includes modules from HL (literature), HG (linguistics), HH (history), and HY (philosophy). Check the module page on [NTUMods](/mods) for programme-specific restrictions before planning to register.

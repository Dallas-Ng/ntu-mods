## Frequently asked questions

### Why does MAE use the "ME" code instead of "MAE"?

NTU's internal faculty code for the School of Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering is `ME`, while the module code prefix used on most modules is `MA`. The school itself is commonly referred to as MAE in its full name.

### How many MAE modules does NTU offer in AY2026/2027 Semester 1?

NTUMods shows approximately 49 MAE modules for AY2026/2027 Semester 1. Check the [Module Browser](/mods?moduleFaculty=ME) for the current live list, since offerings change each semester.

### What is the difference between the general MAE track and Aerospace Engineering?

Both sit under the same school and share foundation modules (dynamics, mechanics of materials, thermodynamics). The aerospace-specific modules — aircraft structures, aerodynamics, propulsion, and air traffic systems — are taken as your programme progresses into its aerospace specialisation. Check your degree audit to confirm which track your programme requires.
